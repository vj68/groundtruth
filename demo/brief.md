# GroundTruth demo brief

## One-sentence story

Engineering leaders are adopting AI faster than their organizations convert change into value;
GroundTruth uses Gemini and a five-agent Google ADK workflow to turn one real failure into a
verified causal lesson, organization-wide protection, and human capability—validated against a
withheld Kubernetes fix and deterministic proof.

## Proof contract

- The agent packet contains the issue and pre-fix source, but not Kubernetes PR #29641.
- Gemini agents propose hypotheses, scope, falsifiers, and interventions.
- Deterministic code—not another model—executes the structural scan and aliasing reproducer.
- The later historical PR is unsealed only after findings are frozen.
- Exact equality between four discovered and four historical paths is the external answer key.
- A SHA-256 append-only chain records how the learning becomes a future control.

## Claim boundary

This is a real retrospective blind benchmark, not a claim that GroundTruth found an unknown
Kubernetes defect. Northstar metrics and future PR NSTR-204 are synthetic product-concept data and
are labeled as such.

## Opening evidence

Google Cloud's 2024 DORA research reports that more than 75% of respondents used AI for at least
one daily professional responsibility. A 25% increase in AI adoption was associated with better
documentation, code quality, and review speed, yet also with 1.5% lower delivery throughput and
7.2% lower delivery stability. Source:
https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/devops-sre/announcing-the-2024-dora-report

The 2025 DORA report describes AI as an amplifier: it magnifies the strengths of high-performing
organizations and the dysfunctions of struggling ones. Source:
https://research.google/pubs/dora-2025-state-of-ai-assisted-software-development-report/
